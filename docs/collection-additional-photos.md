# Collections — additional photograph captions

Implemented 20 September 2026. All 10 supplied photographs (182.jpg–191.jpg) appear in **From the archive** on Collections. Original files are unchanged; web derivatives preserve their full proportions. The following English descriptions are shown in the enlarged reader.

## 182.jpg — Portrait of Mitch Miller

Type: Portrait record. Date: 1991.

A photographed record of Yi Kai’s oil portrait of Mitch Miller. Seated beside an open musical score, the subject gestures with both hands against a warm, softly worked background. The handwritten caption dates the painting to 1991 and records its dimensions as 30 × 40 inches.

## 183.jpg — Portrait of Dolly Fiterman

Type: Portrait record.

Dolly Fiterman is shown seated in a red patterned dress, with a painting behind her. The handwritten note identifies the sitter and records an oil painting measuring 55 × 58 inches. The photograph carries a 2003 date stamp; the painting’s completion year is not recorded here.

Type: Magazine spread. Date: 1993.

## Editorial decisions

- Photographic date stamps are distinguished from painting completion dates.
- Portrait subjects are identified from written captions, not facial recognition.
- Publication covers and facing-page artworks are attributed separately.
- Exhibition certificates retain the joint attribution to Yi Kai and Zhao Tuo.
- Archive photographs do not establish collection ownership. The 16 source collection records are listed separately.
- Original Chinese text printed within scans is retained; all interface text and introductions are English.
